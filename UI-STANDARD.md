# Farouk Collective Web App UI Conformance
## Napkin AI × Gamma AI-inspired

Use the portfolio-wide UI standard in `faroukpandor.github.io/PORTFOLIO-WIDE-UI-DESIGN-STANDARD.md` as the governing design direction.

### Required product experience
- Goal → Source/Idea → Structure → Evidence → Visualise → Decide → Act → Share
- editorial, spacious, narrative-first layouts;
- visual thinking for relationships, flows, comparisons and evidence;
- presentation-quality output/share modes;
- progressive disclosure;
- clear primary action;
- accessible semantic fallbacks;
- responsive/mobile-first behaviour;
- domain-specific identity rather than cloning another product.

### Shared conceptual primitives
Workspace/Canvas, StoryCard, VisualNode, EvidenceChip, SourceBadge, WorkflowStep, InsightPanel, DecisionCard, OutputMode, Dossier/Presentation Renderer.

### Important boundary
Take inspiration from visual thinking and storytelling patterns associated with Napkin AI and Gamma. Do not copy their screens, branding, proprietary assets, code, or distinctive trade dress.

### Engineering rule
Keep the experience portable, lightweight and maintainable. Prefer semantic HTML and simple visual primitives before heavy canvas/runtime dependencies. Preserve the host app's existing architecture unless a migration is justified by product requirements.

### Release gate
A feature is UI-conformant when its purpose is immediately understandable, the user journey is obvious, visualisation adds meaning, evidence is distinguishable from inference, outputs are useful/shareable, accessibility is maintained, and the app retains its own enterprise identity.
